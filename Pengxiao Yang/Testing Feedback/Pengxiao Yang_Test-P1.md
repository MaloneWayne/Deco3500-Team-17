## Appendix C — Post-test sheet

**Q1.** I could raise a concern in this flow without feeling like I was accusing a housemate.  
Score (1–5): **5**  
Why: The swipe mechanism makes it feel like an item choice rather than calling someone out.

**Q2.** I believed the veto (if any) stayed anonymous.  
Score (1–5 or N/A): **4**  
Why: The screen didn't show names, but in a small 4-person house you might still guess by process of elimination.

**Q3.** I understood how my final amount was calculated.  
Score (1–5): **5**  
Why: The locked breakdown listed every item card I claimed or shared.

**Q4.** Claim / pass on each item was better than splitting the whole bill equally.  
Score (1–5): **5**  
Why: Itemizing stops people from absorbing costs for things they didn't consume.

**Q5.** The spinner was an acceptable way to deal with something nobody claimed.  
Score (1–5): **4**  
Why: Good for low-cost communal orphans like cleaning spray.

**Q6.** Trading a chore for part of the bill would be OK in my house if everyone has to accept it.  
Score (1–5): **5**  
Why: As long as the rest of the house votes to accept the chore, it's fair.

**Q7.** Doing this together in one sitting would beat a payment request that arrives later on my phone.  
Score (1–5): **5**  
Why: Settle it in 5 minutes together and forget about it for the rest of the week.

**Q8.** After using this, talking about this week’s bill would feel less awkward than usual in my house.  
Score (1–5): **4**  
Why: The app acts as a buffer and directs attention to the screen rather than face-to-face conflict.

**Q9.** What is the one moment you would refuse to do with real housemates?

**Q10.** What is the one moment you would keep?

> The anonymous Veto card and the pizza proportion slider.

**Q11.** If your house used this next Sunday, what would have to be true (everyone home, one receipt, trust, phones…)?

> All 4 housemates physically sitting in the living room with the paper receipt ready.

**Q12.** Anything missing that would stop you finishing the ritual?

> An automatic Venmo/PayID deep link button at the final locked screen.
