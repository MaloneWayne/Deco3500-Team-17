## Appendix C — Post-test sheet

**Q1.** I could raise a concern in this flow without feeling like I was accusing a housemate.  
Score (1–5): **5**  
Why: The Veto button acts as an anonymous circuit breaker.

**Q2.** I believed the veto (if any) stayed anonymous.  
Score (1–5 or N/A): **4**  
Why: The UI didn't expose my name, though I'd want absolute confirmation on the initial setup screen.

**Q3.** I understood how my final amount was calculated.  
Score (1–5): **5**  
Why: Every item I claimed or passed was reflected line-by-line.

**Q4.** Claim / pass on each item was better than splitting the whole bill equally.  
Score (1–5): **5**  
Why: Prevents the default payer from absorbing everyone else's extras.

**Q5.** The spinner was an acceptable way to deal with something nobody claimed.  
Score (1–5): **3**  
Why: Works fine for low-value house supplies, but shouldn't be used for personal groceries or big bills.

**Q6.** Trading a chore for part of the bill would be OK in my house if everyone has to accept it.  
Score (1–5): **3**  
Why: Depends on the house dynamics; negotiating chore cash values can introduce a new point of argument.

**Q7.** Doing this together in one sitting would beat a payment request that arrives later on my phone.  
Score (1–5): **4**  
Why: Eliminates the passive-aggressive WhatsApp messages later in the week.

**Q8.** After using this, talking about this week’s bill would feel less awkward than usual in my house.  
Score (1–5): **4**  
Why: Shifts the focus onto a shared UI ritual rather than personal demands.

**Q9.** What is the one moment you would refuse to do with real housemates?

> Assigning cash values to chores if people disagree on labor worth.

**Q10.** What is the one moment you would keep?

> The item-by-item swipe claim/pass and the pizza percentage slider.

**Q11.** If your house used this next Sunday, what would have to be true (everyone home, one receipt, trust, phones…)?

**Q12.** Anything missing that would stop you finishing the ritual?

> An option to custom-edit split percentages manually if the slider doesn't cover a complex case.
