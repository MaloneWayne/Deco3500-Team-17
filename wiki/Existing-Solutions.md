# Existing Solutions

## How houses split now

This is not a guess. P01–P06 already named the stack.

**One person and a chat screenshot.** Fast for whoever paid. P02 gets a Messenger number. P03 gets WeChat images. P05 said old expenses vanish in the thread. The house inherits that person’s idea of “fair,” and often cannot reconstruct the line items.

**Splitwise and similar apps.** P04: they work for recording payments. They do not help decide what should be shared. “The difficult part happens before entering the expense.”

**“Just send it on PayID.”** The transfer is easy. The argument about *who owes what* still happened in the hallway, or did not happen, which is worse.

**Equal split by default.** P01, P02, P03, P05. Easy. Then someone eats $10 extra and remembers it.

**Do nothing and seethe.** Named, almost word for word, by P06: “sometimes you choose peace instead of perfect fairness.”

**Informal labour.** Cook for a few nights (P02). Clean the bathroom instead of paying (P05). Never priced. Works until someone wants a rate.

## Why these fail the actual problem

They all assume the hard part is **remembering the number**. It is not.

The hard part is saying, in front of someone you live with, that their protein powder is not a household staple. P04 did say it. They felt like they were accusing a housemate. Current tools leave that speech act to the human. Then they only store the result.

They also train an expectation: money talk is **async, individual, and slightly shameful**. Open the app alone. Get a red badge. Pay. Close it. P01: the stressful part is a $30–$40 message when you cannot remember what it was for.

A good solution for this kitchen would invert that. Same room. Short. The system asks the rude question so the housemate does not have to.

## What this tells us about expectations

People already know how to swipe, vote, and split a bill. They have seen Splitwise. They already use humour and chance (rock-paper-scissors, joke wheels) when a decision gets sticky.

They will expect:

- item-level control, not only a total
- a final “who pays whom” they can trust (P06)
- something they can finish in one sitting (P06 already likes Sunday)

They will not expect a lecture about financial literacy. They will bounce if the house has to nominate a treasurer. They will bounce if chores get a hidden hourly rate.
