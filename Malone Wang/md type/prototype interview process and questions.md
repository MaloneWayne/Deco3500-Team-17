#### Feature 1: The Receipt "Draft" (Tinder for Groceries)

_Show Screen: The Receipt Draft (\$12.00 Fancy Truffle Cheese, Pass \[X\] / Claim \[✓\])_

- **Comprehension Check:**
  - "Looking at this screen without me explaining it, what do you think is happening here, and what would you do next?"
- **Intended Context:** Everyone in the house sits down on Sunday; items from the scanned receipt appear on everyone's screen simultaneously; you swipe/tap Claim or Pass; costs split among whoever claimed it.
- **Formative & Social Questions:**
  - If you saw an item you only took a tiny bite of, would you tap Claim or Pass? Why?
  - How would you feel seeing that 3 of your housemates claimed an item, but you didn't? Would you feel pressured to claim it?
  - What happens if two people genuinely disagree on whether an item was shared? Does swiping bypass the conversation, or just postpone it?

#### Feature 2: The "Icebreaker" Veto & The Review Phase

_Show Screens in sequence: Pre-Lock Review \$\\rightarrow\$ Veto Played! \$\\rightarrow\$ Blind Item Vote OR Friday Night Pizza Slider_

- **Comprehension Check (Screen 1: Pre-Lock Review):**
  - "What do you think the 'Veto Tokens' are for? What would make you press 'Play Veto Card' instead of 'Looks Fair'?"
- **Comprehension Check (Screen 2: Veto Played! - 2:00 Table Talk):**
  - "Your screen turns red and this countdown starts. What do you imagine the room feels like right now?"
- **Comprehension Check (Screen 3: Blind Item Vote & Pizza Slider):**
  - "How do you think the voting and the slider resolve the issue?"
- **Intended Context:** Veto is an anonymous trigger with limited monthly tokens that prompts a co-located 2-minute chat or an anonymous reclassification vote (Communal vs. Personal) or portion slider.
- **Formative & Social Questions:**
  - **Anonymity vs. Paranoia:** The veto trigger is anonymous, but if there are only 3–4 people in the room, can people easily guess who called it? Would that create passive-aggressive tension?
  - **The 2-Minute Timer:** Does the 2-minute "Table Talk" timer help you break the ice, or does it feel confrontational?
  - **Blind Voting:** If Housemate D's item gets voted as "Personal" by the rest of the house (3 against 1), how would Housemate D feel? Does blind voting feel fair, or does it feel like getting ganged up on?
  - **Slider Honesty:** On the Pizza slider ("Just a Nibble" vs "Feral"), do you trust your housemates to be honest? What prevents someone from always picking "Just a Nibble" to save money?

#### Feature 3: The Barter & Penalty System (Chores for Debt)

_Show Screen: House Bounty Proposal (Bounty Card: Bin Duty & Recycling to offset \$10 debt)_

- **Comprehension Check:**
  - "What is Housemate B offering here, and what is your immediate reaction to voting on this?"
- **Intended Context:** A housemate short on cash offers physical labor/chores to forgive a portion of their bill, subject to house vote.
- **Formative & Social Questions:**
  - Would you personally accept household chores in exchange for paying real money out of your pocket? Why or why not?
  - How would you decide if taking out bins for 2 weeks is worth \$10? What happens if they don't do the chore after the debt is cleared?
  - Would proposing this make someone feel embarrassed about their financial situation, or does it feel empowering?

#### Feature 4: The "Orphan Item" Penalty Game

_Show Screen: Unclaimed Item / Spin for the House (\$14 Dishwasher Pods, Wheel with names)_

- **Comprehension Check:**
  - "What do you think happened to cause this screen to appear? How do you feel about the wheel deciding?"
- **Intended Context:** An item on the receipt was rejected by everyone (e.g., shared cleaning supplies nobody claimed). A 10-second minigame picks one person to absorb the cost or do a chore.
- **Formative & Social Questions:**
  - For essential communal items like dishwasher pods or toilet paper, does turning payment into a roulette wheel feel funny, or does it feel unfair if a broke student loses?
  - Can you imagine a situation where someone deliberately refuses to claim an item just to gamble on the wheel?
  - Would this resolve an argument, or cause an argument right at the table?
