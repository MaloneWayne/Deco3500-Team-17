## Contribution to the Design

The research was used to connect participant behaviour to the current prototype direction, particularly:

- private mobile input;
- proportional claim;
- synchronous co-located settlement;
- anonymous review;
- structured mediation of fairness.

The overall research suggests that the design opportunity is not simply to calculate shared expenses more efficiently, but to make disagreement and fairness negotiation easier to express within an ongoing housemate relationship.
